# Code of conduct

Tandem is a small project, and it should be a friendly place to ask questions, report problems
and contribute.

## Expected

- Be respectful and patient, especially with people who are new.
- Assume good intent, and keep criticism about the work, not the person.
- Keep discussions on topic and in good faith.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, personal attacks or discriminatory language.
- Sharing someone else's private information.
- Posting other people's sign-in data, tokens or account details, or asking for them.
- Using the project's spaces to promote ways around a service's terms of use.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close discussions, and block people who do not
follow this code. To report a problem, contact the maintainer through
[GitHub](https://github.com/Arman-Manteghi) or, for anything sensitive, through a
[private security advisory](https://github.com/Arman-Manteghi/Tandem/security/advisories/new).
Reports are handled confidentially.

This code is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/),
version 2.1.
